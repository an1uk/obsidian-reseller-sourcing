- Visit drafts retain their save identity across reopening. Completing draft cleanup cannot duplicate a visit or replay later assessment changes; interrupted outing saves retain newer drafts.

- Filtered maps resolve driving destinations from the complete notebook, including shared and preferred parking. Unsaved capture previews retain their own pins and navigation.
- Google listing identities remain usable when destination coordinates are missing. Exact matching, refreshes and older receipts remain compatible without inferring coordinates.
- New-source capture archival preserves raw bytes and backups, with rollback and retry that do not recreate a source or reapply its details.
- Unnamed place drafts retain contacts, partial coordinates and source-type choices. Back navigation and restored screen state follow reviewed place and notebook moves.
- Nearby criteria survive immediate outing handoff; 24-hour opening works across midnight, and new outings use the selected local date for their folder.

Existing records, preferences and example packs are preserved. The shared plugin files support desktop and Android; Clipper exports remain v0.4.2.
